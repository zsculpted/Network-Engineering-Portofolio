## About Me

I am an International Relations graduate pivoting into Network Engineering, driven by a genuine interest in how computer networks operate and connect. Although my academic background is outside the IT field, I decided to pursue this career path through independent study. This commitment led me to **achieve** the **Cisco Certified Network Associate (CCNA)** certification, marking an important milestone in my transition into networking.

My technical foundation covers networking fundamentals, including TCP/IP, IPv4, IPv6, and subnetting; routing and switching technologies such as static routing, OSPF, EIGRP, VLANs, STP, and EtherChannel; as well as essential network services and security concepts, including DHCP, DNS, NAT, ACLs, SSH, and port security.

In addition to my CCNA, I hold **Fortinet NSE 1–3 certifications**. I am currently aiming to acquire NSE 4 certification and expanding my knowledge of FortiGate firewall configuration, VPN technologies, SD-WAN, and multi-vendor networking through continued study and practical lab exercises.
